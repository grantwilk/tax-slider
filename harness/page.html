What you contribute

Money into a traditional 401(k) lowers income tax, but not Social Security or Medicare tax. Money into an HSA from your pay lowers both. The spread is the discount you get when you buy shares under an incentive stock option. It raises the AMT measure only.

Other situations

A 2025 law created new deductions for tips, for overtime, and for interest on a loan for a new car. Each one has its own income range. Enter an amount and its rule appears on the chart.
